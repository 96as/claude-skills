# Authoritative Saudi Shariah Lists — Where to Find Them

## Why Multiple Lists Exist

Different Shariah supervisory boards apply slightly different methodologies. A stock might be on Al Rajhi's list but not Almaqasid's. The differences are usually:
- Tobacco treatment (some include if < 5%, others exclude entirely)
- Hotel companies with bar revenue (case-by-case judgment)
- Insurance subsidiaries (some accept if Takaful-only)
- Borderline ratios (some round to 30.0%, others reject at 29.5%)

**Best practice:** Pick ONE board whose methodology you trust and stick with it. Cross-checking is good for borderline cases.

## Primary Lists (in order of accessibility)

### 1. Argaam (الأرقام)
- **URL:** https://www.argaam.com/en/company/shariahcompanies (English)
- **Arabic:** https://www.argaam.com/ar/company/shariahcompanies
- **Methodology:** AAOIFI SS21
- **Frequency:** Quarterly (after each earnings release)
- **Coverage:** All Tadawul Main Market + Nomu
- **Cost:** Free
- **Strengths:** Most popular, easy to navigate, also has individual company pages with the latest ratios
- **Use it for:** Default daily check

### 2. Almaqasid (المقاصد)
- **URL:** https://www.almaqasid.com
- **Methodology:** AAOIFI SS21 with senior-scholars committee oversight
- **Frequency:** Quarterly
- **Coverage:** Saudi + GCC stocks
- **Cost:** Some features paid
- **Strengths:** Most conservative; trusted by religious scholars
- **Use it for:** Second opinion when Argaam classifies something as borderline

### 3. Al Rajhi Capital Shariah List
- **URL:** Available within Al Rajhi Tadawul brokerage app/portal
- **Methodology:** Al Rajhi Shariah Board (AAOIFI-aligned)
- **Frequency:** Quarterly
- **Coverage:** Tadawul stocks
- **Cost:** Free for Al Rajhi customers
- **Strengths:** Embedded in trading flow; highly trusted
- **Use it for:** If you trade with Al Rajhi, this is your default

### 4. AlBilad Capital Shariah List
- **URL:** Available within AlBilad brokerage app/portal
- **Methodology:** AlBilad Shariah Board
- **Frequency:** Quarterly
- **Coverage:** Tadawul stocks
- **Cost:** Free for AlBilad customers

### 5. AlInma Investment Shariah List
- Similar to above; available to AlInma customers via their brokerage app.

### 6. ICAP Saudi Capital Shariah List
- **URL:** https://icap.com.sa (publishes a downloadable PDF list)
- **Methodology:** AAOIFI SS21
- **Coverage:** Saudi stocks
- **Cost:** Free PDF download

## What to Do When Lists Disagree

Example scenario: Argaam says compliant. Almaqasid says non-compliant.

**Decision tree:**
1. Read why each list classified it that way (usually one ratio is borderline).
2. If you can verify the underlying data yourself (annual report), do so.
3. When in doubt → follow the **stricter** list. Avoiding doubt (شبهة) is part of Islamic ethics.
4. If still buying, **document your reasoning** so you can re-evaluate next quarter.

## Updating Frequency Matters

Tadawul-listed companies report **quarterly**. Shariah lists therefore update ~4 times/year:
- After Q1 (around April-May)
- After Q2 (around July-August)
- After Q3 (around October-November)
- After Q4 / annual (around February-March)

If your last check was > 3 months ago, **re-verify** before any new purchase.

## A Note on Aramco (2222)

Saudi Aramco is the most-watched Saudi stock. It is generally classified as **compliant with purification** because:
- Core business: oil/gas (permissible)
- Has some interest-bearing debt and cash deposits
- Non-permissible income from interest is small (typically < 2%)
- Always check current ratios before each purchase
