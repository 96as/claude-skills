# Prohibited & Borderline Sectors — Detailed Guidance

## Hard Prohibitions (Tier 1 — Auto-fail)

These business activities make a company NON-compliant regardless of financial ratios:

### 1. Conventional Banking
- **Why:** Core business is interest (riba)
- **Examples:** SNB (1010), Riyad Bank (1020), SABB (1060), ANB (1080), Banque Saudi Fransi (1050)
- **Exception:** Fully Islamic banks — Al Rajhi (1120), AlBilad (1140), AlInma (1150)

### 2. Conventional Insurance
- **Why:** Contains riba, gharar (excessive uncertainty), and maysir (gambling-like elements)
- **Examples:** Most insurance companies on Tadawul
- **Exception:** Takaful companies — operate as cooperative risk-sharing pools (e.g., Al Rajhi Takaful 8230, Jazira Takaful 8012)
- **Note:** Bupa Arabia (8210) — sometimes compliant, depends on structure; verify

### 3. Alcohol
- **Why:** Quranic prohibition
- **Saudi context:** Production/sale of alcohol is illegal in KSA, so direct-alcohol companies are not on Tadawul. The issue arises with hotels and global stocks.

### 4. Pork Products
- **Why:** Quranic prohibition
- **Saudi context:** Not produced in KSA. Issue only with global stocks.

### 5. Gambling, Casinos, Lottery
- **Why:** Maysir is forbidden
- **Examples (global):** Las Vegas Sands, MGM, DraftKings — all non-compliant

### 6. Adult Entertainment
- **Why:** Promotes immorality
- **Includes:** Pornography, dating apps with explicit content

### 7. Conventional Bonds & Riba-Based Finance
- **Why:** Interest-based by design
- **Examples:** Conventional finance companies, leasing companies that charge interest

### 8. Weapons of Mass Destruction
- **Why:** Mass civilian harm
- **Note:** Defense companies that supply legitimate national defense are differently classified by different boards. Conservative position: avoid.

## Borderline Sectors (Tier 2 — Depends on Ratios)

These can be compliant IF non-permissible income < 5% AND financial ratios pass:

### Hotels & Tourism
- **Issue:** May derive revenue from alcohol sales in restaurants/bars
- **Examples on Tadawul:** Dur Hospitality (4010), Tihama (4070)
- **Rule:** If alcohol revenue > 5% of total → NON-compliant. If < 5% → compliant with purification.

### Telecom Companies
- **Issue:** May offer ringtones, content with non-Islamic music, or value-added services
- **Examples:** STC (7010), Mobily (7020), Zain KSA (7030)
- **Generally compliant:** Yes, all three above are usually on Shariah lists.

### Airlines
- **Issue:** May serve alcohol on flights, have interest-bearing aircraft leases
- **Generally compliant:** If interest-bearing debt < 30% (often a challenge for asset-heavy airlines)

### Media & Entertainment
- **Issue:** Content quality, music, advertising for non-permissible products
- **Examples on Tadawul:** MBC Group (4072) — case-by-case
- **Note:** Some boards exclude all media; others assess content

### Real Estate Developers
- **Issue:** Mostly compliant; watch for interest-bearing debt levels
- **Examples:** Dar Al Arkan (4300), Emaar Economic City (4220)

### Healthcare
- **Generally compliant:** Yes — Mouwasat (4002), Dr. Sulaiman Al Habib (4013), Dallah Healthcare (4004)
- **Watch:** Cash-heavy balance sheets exceeding 30% in interest-bearing deposits

### REITs
- **Generally compliant:** Most Saudi REITs are structured Islamic by CMA rules
- **Watch:** Some may have conventional financing in underlying properties

### Petrochemicals
- **Generally compliant:** Core business is permissible
- **Examples:** SABIC (2010), Saudi Kayan (2350), Petrochem (2002)
- **Watch:** Debt ratios — petrochem companies are capital-intensive

## What Counts as "Non-Permissible Income"?

For a company that passes business-activity screening, **non-permissible income** typically includes:

1. **Interest income** on cash deposits at conventional banks (most common)
2. **Conventional insurance receipts** (if not core business)
3. **Penalty fees and late charges** with interest component
4. **Investments in interest-bearing instruments** (bonds, T-bills)
5. **Income from minority subsidiaries** in non-permissible business
6. **Treasury operations** profits from FX speculation

NOT counted as non-permissible:
- Dividends from compliant subsidiaries
- Sukuk income
- Profit from Murabaha or Ijarah financing
- Trade discounts and operating gains
- FX gains from operational currency exchange (case-by-case)
