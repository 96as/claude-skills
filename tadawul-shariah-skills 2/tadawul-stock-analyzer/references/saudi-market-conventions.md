# Saudi Market (Tadawul) — Trading Conventions

## Trading Hours

**Trading Days:** Sunday through Thursday (closed Friday & Saturday)

**Sessions (Riyadh time, AST = GMT+3):**
| Phase | Time |
|---|---|
| Pre-Open Auction | 09:30 – 10:00 |
| Continuous Trading | 10:00 – 15:00 |
| Pre-Close Auction | 15:00 – 15:10 |
| Closing Price Determination | 15:10 |
| Trade-At-Close | 15:10 – 15:20 |

**Market closes** during major Islamic holidays (Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha) — sometimes for a full week.

## Settlement

- **T+2** for equities (trade today, settle in 2 business days)
- This means cash from sales is available T+2
- Buying requires either cash or margin (margin must be Shariah-compliant if you require it)

## Daily Price Limits

| Market | Limit |
|---|---|
| Main Market (TASI) — established stocks | ±10% per day |
| Main Market — IPO first 3 days | ±30% / day, no limit on day 1 close |
| Nomu Parallel Market | ±30% per day |
| Sukuk & Bonds | ±10% |

**Implication for short-term traders:** A stock can be "limit-up" or "limit-down" — meaning trading is paused once the daily limit is hit. You can't buy at limit-up easily.

## Lot Sizes

- Most stocks trade in single shares (lot = 1)
- Some Nomu stocks may have larger minimum lots — verify

## Order Types Supported

- **Market Order** — fill at best available
- **Limit Order** — specify price
- **Iceberg** — large order revealed gradually
- **Fill or Kill (FOK)** — full fill or cancel
- **Immediate or Cancel (IOC)** — partial fill ok, cancel rest

## Foreign Ownership Limits (FOL)

- Most stocks: 49% foreign ownership cap (varies)
- Some sensitive sectors: lower limits (e.g., banks, telecom)
- Saudi Aramco: very low foreign limit (most shares are local)

## Short Selling

- Allowed under Securities Borrowing & Lending (SBL) framework, but limited list of eligible securities
- **Short selling is generally NOT Shariah-compliant** (sale of what you don't own — gharar) — most Islamic boards prohibit it
- Recommendation for Shariah-conscious investors: **avoid short selling**

## Margin Trading

- Available through brokers
- **Conventional margin = riba** (interest-bearing) → NON-compliant
- **Shariah-compliant margin** is offered by Islamic brokers via Murabaha structures — verify with your broker
- Recommendation: avoid margin unless you've verified the structure with your Shariah advisor

## Market Indices

| Index | What It Tracks |
|---|---|
| **TASI** | All Tadawul Main Market stocks (broad benchmark) |
| **MT30** | Top 30 by float-adjusted market cap |
| **MSCI Tadawul 30** | MSCI's version of large-caps |

For Shariah investing: There are also Saudi Shariah-screened indices such as the **MSCI Saudi Arabia Islamic Index** and **S&P Saudi Arabia Domestic Shariah Index**.

## Major Announcements & Calendar

Saudi companies must disclose:
- Quarterly earnings (within 30 days of quarter-end)
- Annual reports (within 90 days of year-end)
- Material events (immediately)
- Board changes
- Major contracts (over a threshold)
- Dividend announcements
- AGM resolutions

**Where to find:** Saudi Exchange "Disclosures" section, or Argaam news feed.

## Tax Considerations

- **No capital gains tax** for Saudi residents on Saudi stocks
- **No dividend withholding tax** for Saudi residents
- **Foreign investors:** May face withholding (varies by treaty)
- **Zakat:** Saudi citizens and GCC nationals owe zakat on wealth (calculated annually); shares held for trading are zakatable on market value, shares held for long-term investment have different rules — consult a scholar.

## Currency

- All trading in **Saudi Riyal (SAR)**
- SAR is **pegged to USD at 3.75** (very stable since 1986)
- This reduces FX risk but means Saudi monetary policy effectively follows the Fed

## Liquidity Considerations

- TASI top names (Aramco, Al Rajhi, SABIC, STC) have deep liquidity — easy to enter/exit
- Mid-cap names: moderate liquidity, watch the bid-ask spread
- Small-cap and Nomu names: thin liquidity, can be hard to exit large positions
- **Avoid stocks with average daily value < SAR 1 million if you plan position sizes over SAR 100K**

## Volatility Reference

- TASI annualized volatility historically ~15-20% (lower than emerging-market peers in EM)
- Individual stocks: small caps can move 5-10%/day routinely
- Single-day ±10% limit caps extreme moves (forces volatility into multiple days)

## Weekend Gap Risk

Because the market is closed Friday-Saturday, news from Western markets (Friday close to Sunday open) creates a "weekend gap":
- Oil price moves on Friday-Saturday hit oil-linked Saudi stocks Sunday morning
- US market events Friday-Saturday can drive Sunday opening
- **Key for short-term traders:** Manage position size or hedge before weekend if there's news risk

## Sunday Special

Tadawul opens on **Sunday** (when most global markets are closed). This means:
- Saudi market often "leads" the global week
- Sunday gap up/down sets sector tone for the week
- Useful for early read on Mideast/oil sentiment
