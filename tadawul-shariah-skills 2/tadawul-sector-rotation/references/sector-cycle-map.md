# Saudi Sector — Market Cycle Map

## Standard Business Cycle Phases

### Phase 1: Early Cycle (Recovery)
**Macro signs:**
- Interest rates falling or low
- PMI rising from contraction
- Consumer confidence improving
- Commodities recovering
- Credit spreads tightening

**Best Saudi sectors (Shariah-compliant):**
- 🟢 Materials (cement, mining) — Maaden, cement names
- 🟢 Consumer Discretionary (retail, autos) — Jarir, Extra
- 🟢 Real Estate — Dar Al Arkan, REITs
- 🟢 Industrials — construction-linked
- 🟢 Petchem (early stages of recovery) — SABIC, Kayan

**Sectors to underweight:**
- 🔴 Defensives (utilities, staples) — they lag the recovery
- 🔴 Cash (you're missing the rally)

### Phase 2: Mid Cycle (Expansion)
**Macro signs:**
- Stable rates or slight rise
- PMI consistently expansion
- Earnings growth broad
- Capex picks up
- Risk appetite high

**Best Saudi sectors:**
- 🟢 Telecom (data demand growth) — STC, Mobily, Zain
- 🟢 Healthcare — Mouwasat, Al Habib, Dallah
- 🟢 Industrials — diversified industrial
- 🟢 Tech (limited on Tadawul but growing)
- 🟢 Petchem (mid-expansion is sweet spot)

**Watch for:**
- Sector dispersion increases — stock-picking starts to matter more

### Phase 3: Late Cycle (Peak Approaching)
**Macro signs:**
- Rates rising
- Inflation pressure
- PMI plateau or slowing
- Yield curve flattening (US — affects SAMA mirror)
- Energy/commodities firm

**Best Saudi sectors:**
- 🟢 Energy — Aramco, refiners (oil typically peaks late cycle)
- 🟢 Materials/Mining — commodities cycle
- 🟢 Healthcare (start defensive shift)
- 🟢 Consumer Staples (Almarai, food names)

**Reduce exposure to:**
- 🟡 Cyclical Industrials
- 🟡 High-multiple growth names
- 🟡 Real Estate (rate-sensitive)

### Phase 4: Recession / Contraction
**Macro signs:**
- Falling earnings
- Rising unemployment
- Credit spreads widening
- Defensive bid

**Best Saudi sectors (defensive):**
- 🟢 Consumer Staples — Almarai, Othaim, food
- 🟢 Healthcare — Mouwasat, Al Habib
- 🟢 Telecom — STC, Mobily
- 🟢 Utilities — limited Shariah-compliant options
- 🟢 Cash — preservation of capital

**Avoid:**
- 🔴 Cyclical names
- 🔴 Highly-leveraged names (debt cost up)
- 🔴 Discretionary retail

## Saudi-Specific Cycle Considerations

### Oil Cycle (Often Drives Saudi Cycle)
The Saudi business cycle is heavily influenced by oil prices:
- **Oil rising → Government revenue up → Fiscal expansion → Economy expansion → Bank loan demand → Materials/petchem strong**
- **Oil falling → Fiscal tightening → Slower growth → Defensive rotation**

This can mean Saudi sectors don't always follow US/global cycle — they follow oil first.

### Vision 2030 Mega-Cycle (2017-2030)
Independent of business cycle, sovereign spending on giga-projects creates persistent tailwinds:
- **Construction & Cement** — multi-year build phase
- **Mining** — diversification away from oil
- **Healthcare** — capacity expansion
- **Tourism / Hospitality** — Red Sea, NEOM, AlUla
- **Logistics** — port expansion, trade hub strategy
- **Renewables** — solar, hydrogen (ACWA Power)

### Hajj/Ramadan Seasonal Cycle
Annual seasonal effects on:
- **Telecom** — peak data usage during Hajj season
- **Hotels/Hospitality** — Hajj season inflows
- **Retail/F&B** — Ramadan consumption patterns
- **Transport** — pilgrim movement

## Practical Action Framework

### When you've identified the cycle phase:

**Don't** flip your entire portfolio overnight — that's overconfidence. Instead:

1. **Tilt** sector weights gradually (5-10% shifts, not 50%)
2. **Trim** sectors moving against the cycle
3. **Add** to sectors aligned with the cycle on pullbacks (not at highs)
4. **Hold** core Shariah-compliant names regardless of cycle (Aramco, Al Rajhi as anchors)
5. **Cash up** if cycle is unclear or transitional

### When you're WRONG about the cycle:
- Be willing to revise. Cycle calls are probabilistic, not deterministic.
- Use TASI breadth as a feedback loop — if TASI breaks down despite your "early cycle" call, downgrade
- Don't average down on sector ETFs aggressively if your thesis is breaking

## Common Cycle Mistakes

1. **Calling the bottom too early** — Recoveries often have multiple false starts
2. **Calling the top too early** — Bull markets can run on past "peak signals" for months
3. **Ignoring Vision 2030 floor** — Some Saudi sectors have sovereign support that overrides natural cycle
4. **Following US cycle blindly** — Saudi has unique drivers; US is one input, not the whole picture
5. **Over-rotating** — Trading costs and emotional whiplash hurt returns more than missing the rotation
