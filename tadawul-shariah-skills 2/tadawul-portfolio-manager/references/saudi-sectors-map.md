# Tadawul Sector Classification Map

The Saudi Exchange uses TASI sector classifications. Here's a practical map of each sector with sample tickers and Shariah notes.

## Energy
- **Description:** Oil, gas, refining
- **Shariah notes:** Generally compliant
- **Key names:** 2222 Aramco, 2030 Saudi Refining (SARCO), 2381 Arabian Drilling
- **Drivers:** Oil price (Brent), OPEC+ decisions, refining margins

## Materials (Petrochemicals & Chemicals & Mining)
- **Description:** Petrochemicals, basic chemicals, mining
- **Shariah notes:** Generally compliant; watch debt levels
- **Key names:** 2010 SABIC, 2350 Saudi Kayan, 2380 Petro Rabigh, 1211 Maaden, 2300 SPM
- **Drivers:** Oil prices, global PMI, methanol/MEG/PE prices, copper/gold (Maaden)

## Capital Goods (Industrials)
- **Key names:** 4140 Saudi Industrial Development (SIDCO), 1320 Saudi Steel Pipe, 1301 United Wire
- **Drivers:** Vision 2030 construction, infrastructure spending

## Commercial & Professional Services
- **Key names:** 1820 Sadr Logistics, 9534 SAL Saudi Logistics
- **Drivers:** Trade volume, logistics demand

## Transportation
- **Key names:** 4030 Bahri (shipping), 4031 SGS (Saudi Ground Services), 4040 Saudi Airlines
- **Shariah notes:** Airlines often have debt issues; verify case-by-case

## Consumer Durables & Apparel
- **Key names:** 4011 Lazurde (jewelry), various textile

## Consumer Services
- **Key names:** 4170 TECO (Trade Center), 4071 Cenomi Centers, 4010 Dur Hospitality
- **Shariah notes:** Hotels with alcohol revenue can be flagged — verify

## Media & Entertainment
- **Key names:** 4072 MBC Group, 4070 Tihama
- **Shariah notes:** Content type matters; some boards exclude entirely

## Retailing
- **Key names:** 4190 Jarir, 4003 Extra (United Electronics), 4001 Othaim Markets, 4240 Cenomi Retail
- **Shariah notes:** Generally compliant; verify product mix

## Food & Staples Retailing
- **Key names:** 4001 Othaim Markets, 4006 BinDawood, 4163 Al Dawaa Pharma

## Food, Beverage & Tobacco
- **Key names:** 2280 Almarai, 2050 Savola, 6004 Catering, 6020 GACO Agri
- **Shariah notes:** Tobacco companies excluded; food/dairy fine

## Healthcare Equipment & Services
- **Key names:** 4002 Mouwasat, 4004 Dallah Healthcare, 4005 Care, 4007 Saudi German Health, 4013 Dr. Sulaiman Al Habib, 4014 SMACO
- **Shariah notes:** Generally compliant — Vision 2030 darling sector

## Pharma, Biotech & Life Sciences

## Sector Concentration Visualization

A typical "diversified" Tadawul portfolio breaks down by SECTOR like:

```
[Banks 30%]        [Petchem/Energy 30%]    [Telecom 10%]
[Real Estate 8%]   [Healthcare 8%]         [Consumer 7%]
[Materials 4%]     [Other 3%]
```

Note how dominant Banks and Petchem/Energy are. For Shariah investors:
- Banks shrinks to just Islamic banks (~10-15% feasible)
- Petchem/Energy is roughly the same
- Net result: Shariah Saudi portfolios often LIGHTER on banks, HEAVIER on alternatives like healthcare and consumer

## Sector Beta to Oil

| Sector | Oil Sensitivity |
|---|---|
| Energy (Aramco, refiners) | Very High |
| Petrochemicals | High |
| Banks | Medium (via economic cycle) |
| Real Estate | Medium-Low |
| Telecom | Low |
| Healthcare | Low |
| Consumer staples | Low |
| Utilities | Low |

A portfolio overweight in Energy + Petchem can be 40-50% effectively oil-exposed even with "diversification" by name.
