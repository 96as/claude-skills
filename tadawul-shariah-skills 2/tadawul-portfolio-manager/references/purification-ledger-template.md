# Dividend Purification Ledger Template

## How to Use

1. Each time you receive a dividend from a Tadawul stock, log it here.
2. Multiply by the company's most recent non-permissible income % (from Argaam, Almaqasid, or your screening).
3. Pay the purification amount to charity (sadaqah) — separately from zakat.
4. Mark as paid.

## Reminders

- **Purification ≠ Zakat.** They are separate obligations.
- **Charity recipients:** General sadaqah (poor, needy, public welfare). NOT mosque construction, NOT Quran printing (those earn reward; purification is removing impurity).
- **Cannot give to:** Family members you're financially responsible for.
- **Frequency:** Pay annually at minimum; per-dividend if preferred.
- **Records:** Keep receipts/proof of charitable donations for your records (and for tax in some jurisdictions).
